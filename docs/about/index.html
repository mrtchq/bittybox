Bitty Box

A page, packed into a link.

Write in the editor, then copy or share your link. Bitty Box compresses the page into the URL. Opening that link reconstructs the page in the recipient’s browser.

The core editor needs no account and does not upload your page to a content database. Its content travels in the link itself.

Treat links from unknown senders like any other webpage. Keep private information out of unencrypted links, and remember that anyone with a link can pass it along.
