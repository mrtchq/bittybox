Bitty Boxes are public

A Bitty Box link contains its entire content. It is not private and not encrypted. Anyone who has the link can read it, forward it, or publish it. Do not put private information, secrets, or anything you would not want a stranger to read into an unencrypted link.

If you believe a link hosts content that breaks these terms — malware, phishing, or an infringement of your rights — email support@bittybox.org with the link, and we will review it.

Locked boxes

Bitty Box supports password, time, countdown, deadman, and invite-only locks. These are features of the link format. They deter casual access; they are not a guarantee against a determined recipient, and they are not encryption. A lock protects the destination, not the link itself.

Deadman switches are set to trigger at a time you choose. If a deadman switch triggers, the box becomes openable. Check your configured triggers, because we cannot recover a link sent at the wrong time.
